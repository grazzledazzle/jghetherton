This past Saturday was the **Sinister Story Showdown**, presented by Durham County Library and the Horror Writers Association. Authors were asked to submit their most **_terrifying tale_**, a spine-tingling story to haunt the judges' dreams.

Long story short...I won!

The story, called HAND-ME-DOWN, will be featured in a short story anthology that will be added to the library's collection. Thanks to the Durham County Library and the Horror Writers Association, most especially for the incredible trophy. This one is staying on the mantle long after Halloween ends.
